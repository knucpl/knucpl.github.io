Our first conference all together - 2026 KMS Autumn Meeting 🍂

Created on October 16, 2026

2026

From left: Seokhyun (back) and HyeonSik (front), Yujin, Kwonil, Jungmin, Haeun, Seeun, and Seri

  • Our group attended the 2026 Autumn Meeting of the Korean Meteorological Society (KMS), held on October 14–16, 2026 at the Gyeongju Hwabaek International Convention Center (HICO) in Gyeongju
  • This was the first time that all members of our lab attended a conference together 🙌
  • Four students presented posters, and Kwonil gave an oral presentation
  • Seri received the Outstanding Presentation Award for the poster presentation 🏆 Congratulations! 🎉👏
